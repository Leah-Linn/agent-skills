# Geography and Scoring

## Geography gate

Apply the gate before any weighted score.

| Zone | Markets | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | Europe; Singapore; Malaysia | Research and rank first. Resolve Europe to country/cluster whenever possible. |
| Yellow | Thailand; Vietnam; Indonesia | Do not recommend by default. Allow only a clearly justified, high-quality exception and label it. |
| Hard exclude | Philippines; Cambodia; Myanmar | Never recommend or treat as positive territory exposure. A material share of any broad territory fails the geography gate. |

For broad `SEA`, `ASEAN`, `APAC`, or `International` titles, verify:

1. exact owned countries;
2. location and relocation expectations;
3. revenue/customer distribution by territory;
4. travel frequency and duration;
5. whether Singapore/Malaysia or Europe is a real book of business;
6. whether hard-excluded countries are material.

If territory remains unknown, mark geographic fit `Unverified` and cap the role recommendation at watchlist status.

## Sales Entry Score — 100

Score an industry-chain node, not a company.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Overseas demand in accepted markets | 20 |
| Customer quality | 15 |
| Technical/product barrier | 15 |
| Sales complexity and learning | 15 |
| Typical contract value/economic importance | 15 |
| Channel/ecosystem value | 10 |
| Career transferability | 10 |

Explain each material score. Do not manufacture precision where evidence is weak; use a range when necessary.

## Company Career Fit Score — 100

Apply only after the geography gate.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Europe/Singapore/Malaysia fit | 25 |
| Overseas growth momentum | 15 |
| Product moat | 15 |
| Sales sophistication | 15 |
| Customer quality | 10 |
| Channel/local service maturity | 10 |
| Future career mobility | 10 |

Bands: S = 85–100, A = 75–84, B = 60–74, C = below 60. A hard-excluded territory cannot be rescued by a high total.

## Role Quality Score — 100

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Verified accepted territory ownership | 25 |
| Full-cycle responsibility | 20 |
| Product/solution complexity | 15 |
| Enterprise customer quality | 10 |
| Presales, delivery, and local support | 10 |
| Existing base plus credible growth room | 10 |
| Accessibility for the user's current profile | 10 |

Do not assume salary, quota, deal size, or existing revenue. Record these as interview questions when unknown.

## Keep three rankings separate

1. **Industry/company strength:** competitive position and business quality.
2. **Overseas sales career value:** accepted-market fit and career capital.
3. **Entry realism:** current verified openings and user eligibility.

Never collapse these into one league table. A hidden champion may outrank a famous leader for career value, while a lower-ranked company may be the best graduate entry.
