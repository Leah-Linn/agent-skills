# Job-Market Intelligence

## Search only after defining targets

Build the target nodes, companies, markets, and role keywords first. Then search combinations in Chinese, English, company/local language where useful:

- company + careers/campus/graduate;
- company + international/overseas/regional/EMEA/Europe/APAC sales;
- company + key account/channel/solution/project/business development;
- company + Singapore/Malaysia and specific European countries/clusters;
- relevant Chinese equivalents such as 海外销售、国际业务、欧洲区域、海外大客户、海外渠道、解决方案销售、国际管培生.

Search company career systems directly as well as indexed web results. A missing search result does not prove no opening exists.

## Meet a practical coverage bar

Do not stop after finding a few famous-company roles. For an industry report:

1. Open the official recruiting entry for every A-tier company.
2. Search role families across the product vendor, software/platform, distributor, and integrator layers.
3. Search Europe by specific country/cluster and search Singapore and Malaysia separately; do not rely on one broad `Europe`, `SEA`, or `APAC` query.
4. Search Chinese-headquartered companies for China-based overseas roles in addition to local overseas roles.
5. Continue until a new search round yields no material new target company or live role, or the best available evidence clearly saturates.

Aim for 12–20 verified or likely-live, non-duplicate jobs when the market supports them. This is a coverage target, not a quota: never pad with weak geography, old posts, generic portals, or adjacent roles that do not create relevant career capital. If fewer survive verification, say so plainly in the pre-table market interpretation.

## Enforce source priority

Use this order for recruiting evidence:

1. **Official company career pages and official company ATS/job detail pages.**
2. **Official company campus/graduate recruitment pages and official company recruiting accounts.**
3. **LinkedIn, BOSS直聘, and major job platforms** such as Liepin, Zhaopin, Maimai, JobsDB, JobStreet, Indeed, or locally dominant platforms.
4. **School career pages only as supplemental evidence.** Do not lead with them and do not use them to replace an available official or major-platform link.

Surface a school page when it contains material extra value: referral/internal recommendation code, exclusive application link, school-only session, deadline, recruiter contact, eligibility detail, or a vacancy absent elsewhere. Put that information in the final table's `提醒/独家信息` cell and label the source supplemental.

Corporate press releases, social posts, repost aggregators, and search snippets may help discovery but do not prove a live opening without a working application path or current posting evidence.

## Verify every opening

For each row, record:

- exact company and role title;
- actual territory and work location;
- graduate/experienced eligibility;
- source tier and source publisher;
- posted/updated date if available;
- date checked;
- application status: verified live, likely live, unclear, expired/closed, or verification unavailable;
- direct job/application URL;
- geography gate and role-quality judgment.

Open the page. Do not rely only on snippets. Check for closed/expired notices, broken application buttons, generic talent-community forms, mismatched titles, repost age, and redirects to unrelated job lists.

Distinguish these link types:

- **Company-pool entry:** generic official careers page, ATS home, or company recruiting landing page. Use only in the earlier company pool and label it `关注入口`.
- **Live-job link:** a page for one exact role with matching title/company/location and an active application path. Only this type belongs in `实时招聘结果`.
- **Discovery-only link:** search results, job-list pages, aggregators, snippets, or social reposts without a current application path. Use to find the source, never as proof of a live job.

Deduplicate jobs by company, normalized title, location/territory, and requisition ID. When official and platform copies describe the same role, keep the official direct link. Do not count language mirrors, reposts, or alternate ATS URLs as separate openings.

Use `verified live` only when the page is current and accepts applications or the official system clearly lists the role. Use `likely live` when a reputable current platform lists it but official confirmation is unavailable. Clearly label all uncertainty.

Exclude `expired/closed` from the final table. If the closure itself changes strategy—for example, a priority company recently filled its only regional role—mention it once in the market interpretation before the final section.

## Keep jobs at the absolute end

Every industry report must end with exactly one `## 实时招聘结果` section and one consolidated table. Do not place individual job cards or job tables earlier. Company sections may discuss hiring signals without listing openings.

Required columns, in this order:

| 公司 | 岗位 | 地域/工作地 | 适用人群 | 来源级别 | 状态与核验日期 | 直接链接 | 匹配判断 | 提醒/独家信息 |

Rules:

- Use clickable direct links to one exact JD/application page; never invent URLs.
- Never put a generic careers homepage, ATS search page, company homepage, job-search results page, or talent-community form in the live table.
- Put referral codes and school-only intelligence in the final column.
- Exclude hard-excluded geography from recommended results; disclose the geography rule earlier in the report if it affected coverage.
- If no verified role exists, include one row with company/role as `—`, status `未找到可核验在招岗位（核验日期：YYYY-MM-DD）`, and summarize searched company/source coverage in the final cell.
- If web access is unavailable, use one row marked `无法进行实时核验`; do not recycle stale roles as live.
- End the report immediately after the final table row.

## Interpret the market

After collecting roles—but before the final table—summarize:

- hiring volume and concentration;
- recurring role/skill/language keywords;
- accepted-market coverage quality;
- graduate versus experienced entry supply;
- the gap between attractive companies and actual openings;
- application priorities and alerts.

This analysis must precede the final recruiting section so the table remains the absolute end.
